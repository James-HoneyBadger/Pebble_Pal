# Privacy Policy — Pebble Pal

**Last updated: 26 October 2026**

Pebble Pal is a virtual pet game that runs entirely on your device.

## Data collection

Pebble Pal does **not** collect, transmit, or store any personal information.
There are no accounts, sign-ins, analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking of any
kind, and the app makes no network requests during normal use.

## Data stored on your device

Game progress (your rock's name, stats, level, achievements, stickers, journal
entries and settings) is saved locally using the device's standard web storage
inside the app sandbox. This data never leaves your device and is not readable by
us or by any third party.

## Permissions

- **Vibration / haptics** — used only to give physical feedback when you interact
  with your rock. No data is collected.
- **Photos (optional)** — if you export a rock card and choose to save it, the
  image is written to your photo library by the operating system. The app does not
  read your photo library.

Sound effects are synthesised on device; no audio is recorded or transmitted.

## Data deletion

- **In app** — open ⚙️ Settings → *Reset Save Data*. This erases all progress
  immediately and cannot be undone.
- **Desktop** — Game → *Reset Rock*.
- **Uninstalling** the app removes all associated data from your device.

## Children's privacy

The app collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

## Third-party services

None. Pebble Pal does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising,
or tracking services.
